# Contributing to CAES

CAES is a normative specification. Contributions must preserve structural clarity.

---

## What We Accept

- Clarifications to normative language
- Improvements to determinism requirements
- Evidence integrity enhancements
- Conformance statement refinements
- Identification of loopholes
- Structural ambiguity fixes

---

## What We Do Not Accept

- Vendor-specific requirements
- Marketing language
- Feature expansion unrelated to authorization or evidence
- Non-deterministic enforcement mechanisms

---

## Proposal Format

Amendment proposals must include:

- Affected section
- Normative requirement ID. v0.2.0 does not yet assign stable IDs. Cite the section heading until it does.
- Structural issue description
- Rationale
- Backward compatibility implications

Proposals lacking structural justification will not be considered.

---

## Submission Process

1. Open an Issue labeled `Amendment Proposal`
2. Submit structured proposal per template
3. Participate in discussion
4. Await versioned disposition

CAES does not merge unversioned normative changes.
